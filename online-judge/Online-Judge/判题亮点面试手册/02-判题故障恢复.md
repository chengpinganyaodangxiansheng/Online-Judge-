# 判题故障恢复

## 一、技术实现分析

### 1. 设计动机

Worker 领取任务后可能宕机、长时间暂停或失去数据库连接。系统需要识别失效执行并安排重试，还要防止已失权的 Worker 恢复后覆盖结果。

### 2. 核心实现

1. `JudgeConsumer.consume()` 调 `JudgeTaskServiceImpl.ready()` 校验事件，取到容器后再调 `claim()`。 `JudgeTaskMapper.claim()` 检查状态、派发代次和排队截止时间，写入 `RUNNING`、令牌及两种执行期限，同事务记录 attempt。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)
2. `consume()` 在独立调度线程上续租并设置看门狗。续租失败或固定期限到达时，`stopExecution()` 撤销执行资格、中断线程并尝试终止容器。结果经 `JudgeTaskServiceImpl.finish()` 提交，SQL 再次核对令牌和期限。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/JudgeTaskConfig.java) · [JudgeExecution.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/domain/JudgeExecution.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)
3. `JudgeRecoveryJob.recover()` 扫描候选，`JudgeTaskServiceImpl.recover()` 加行锁复查。过期执行经 `fail()` 进入退避或终态，待重试任务由 `dispatch()` 递增代次，和新 Outbox 事件一起提交。 [JudgeRecoveryJob.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/job/JudgeRecoveryJob.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)
4. `UserQuestionController.retry()` 调用 `JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.retry()`。管理员可给系统失败任务开启新预算，沿用原快照，保留历史并记审计。 [UserQuestionController.java](../oj-modules/oj-friend/src/main/java/org/example/friend/controller/user/UserQuestionController.java) · [JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-friend/src/main/java/org/example/friend/service/user/impl/JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

### 3. 技术原理

派发代次标识这一轮消息，执行令牌标识这一轮执行者。数据库的 `NOW(6)` 统一裁决租约，续租最多延长到固定截止时间。耗时执行在事务外，资格检查和状态变化用短事务完成。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

### 4. 技术取舍

只靠 MQ 重投可以再次收到消息，但无法判断旧 Worker 是否还能写数据库。单独加 Redis 锁则需要协调锁与任务行的生命周期。当前方案让任务行直接裁决领取和写回，代价是续租和定时扫描都依赖数据库，也会增加写入和行锁竞争。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeRecoveryJob.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/job/JudgeRecoveryJob.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

### 5. 局限与风险

有效令牌能挡住旧结果，旧容器尚未终止时仍可能与重试执行短暂重叠。续租、看门狗和暂停恢复共用两线程调度池，慢调用可能拖延回调。故障恢复依赖数据库可用和扫描继续运行，实际恢复时延未确认。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/JudgeTaskConfig.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

## 二、面试问题与参考答案

### 1. 为什么同时需要派发代次和执行令牌？

代次用来识别消息是否属于当前派发，旧消息拿着旧代次就领不到任务。执行令牌是在成功领取时生成的，续租和完成 SQL 要用它证明这次执行仍然有效。两者分别解决旧消息和旧执行者的问题。 [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

### 2. 两个消费者都通过了预检，会同时执行同一轮吗？

预检只表示可以去拿资源。取到容器后，只有一个消费者能用匹配代次把任务从 `RECEIVED` 更新到 `RUNNING`，并记录 attempt。另一个更新不到行，会归还未使用容器并 ACK 这次投递。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

### 3. 为什么有续租，还要设置固定截止时间和看门狗？

续租表明 Worker 仍能访问数据库，无法证明用户程序有进展。SQL 用 `LEAST` 给续租设置上限，固定期限到达后看门狗尝试停止本地执行和容器。即使停止动作延迟，结果写回仍要通过数据库期限检查。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeExecution.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/domain/JudgeExecution.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

### 4. Worker 被杀掉后，下一次执行是怎么触发的？

续租停止，数据库租约过期。恢复扫描加锁复查后终止旧 attempt，若还有预算就进入 `RETRY_WAIT`。当前配置每轮最多三次执行，退避按累计尝试次数指数增长，上限 30 秒。退避结束且排队未超时，恢复事务增加派发代次并写新事件，发布任务再把它送入 MQ。重复消息的预检也可触发过期检查。 [JudgeRecoveryJob.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/job/JudgeRecoveryJob.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java) · [application.yml](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/resources/application.yml)

### 5. 旧 Worker 恢复后把结果提交上来，哪些条件会拦住它？

本地执行可能已经被撤销。即使本地还活着，完成 SQL 也要求 `RUNNING`、令牌一致、租约和固定期限都有效。更新不到一行就不会写提交历史或成绩。旧容器仍在运行属于资源清理问题，由终止和回收路径处理。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java)

### 6. 两个扫描器查到同一条待重试记录，会产生两条新派发吗？

扫描只返回候选。进入恢复事务后先锁任务行，再用状态、代次和截止条件判断能否派发。先成功的事务改变状态和代次并写入事件，后一个重新读到的新状态不再符合派发条件。 [JudgeRecoveryJob.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/job/JudgeRecoveryJob.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

### 7. WA、CE 和沙箱通信断开分别怎么处理？

WA、CE 等是可判定的用户结果，走 `finish()`，最终状态同样是 `FINISHED`。通信断开等平台异常走 `fail()`，按次数决定退避或 `SYSTEM_FAILED`。快照缺失或不合法走 `INVALID_SNAPSHOT`，当前分支直接终结。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeServiceImpl.java) · [SandboxVerdictServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxVerdictServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java)

### 8. 排队期限、租约、固定执行期限和用例时限分别保护什么？

排队期限限制任务能等多久才开始，超时可在领取前终结。租约限制当前 Worker 的有效资格，固定执行期限限制一次 attempt 的最大时间。用例时限由沙箱阶段执行，可能产生 TLE 这个业务结果。 [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java) · [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [SandboxManager.java](../sandbox-runtime/src/SandboxManager.java) · [SandboxSupervisor.java](../sandbox-runtime/src/SandboxSupervisor.java)

### 9. 自动重试用完后，管理员重试会重置哪些数据？

仅系统失败任务可以人工重试，还要通过管理员和比赛状态检查。代码把 `retry_base` 设为当前累计尝试数并递增派发代次，重设排队期限，保存新事件及操作原因。原快照和历史 attempt 继续保留。 [JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-friend/src/main/java/org/example/friend/service/user/impl/JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)
