# 异步判题

## 一、技术实现分析

### 1. 设计动机

一次判题要准备环境、编译并运行多个用例，耗时受用户程序和资源排队影响。提交接口先完成受理，用户凭编号查询结果。请求响应丢失时，用户还能重试同一次提交，避免为一次点击创建两条任务。

### 2. 核心实现

1. `Answer.vue` 的 `submitQuestion()` 冻结请求并生成幂等键，`sendPendingRequest()` 发送或重发，拿到编号后由 `getQuestionResult()` 查询。 [Answer.vue](../../../online-judge-friend-c/oj-c/src/views/Answer.vue)
2. `UserQuestionController.submit()` 处理 `POST /user/question/submit`，调用 `JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.submit()` 查重。新请求由 `accept()` 在同一事务写入任务、执行快照和 `JudgeRequested` Outbox。查询由 `UserQuestionController.task()` 调 `find()`，按当前用户校验归属。 [UserQuestionController.java](../oj-modules/oj-friend/src/main/java/org/example/friend/controller/user/UserQuestionController.java) · [JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-friend/src/main/java/org/example/friend/service/user/impl/JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java) · [006-reliable-judge.sql](../deploy/sql/006-reliable-judge.sql)
3. `JudgeRecoveryJob.publish()` 批量扫描到期候选并交给有界线程池。 `OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.deliver()` 在工作线程条件领取后发送，按 Confirm 和路由退回更新 Outbox。发送端与消费端使用同一持久化判题队列。 [JudgeRecoveryJob.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/job/JudgeRecoveryJob.java) · [OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeOutboxMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeOutboxMapper.java) · [JudgeTaskConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/JudgeTaskConfig.java) · [application.yml](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/resources/application.yml)
4. `JudgeConsumer.consume()` 核对事件并领取任务，从快照执行，调用 `JudgeTaskServiceImpl.finish()` 或 `fail()`。有效结果或失败处理事务返回后再 ACK，过时代次等无需执行的投递也会确认。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java)

### 3. 技术原理

幂等键按用户唯一，请求摘要区分同键不同内容。Outbox 与任务同库提交，先留下可恢复的发送记录，再调用 Broker。发布确认和消费确认分别覆盖生产者到 Broker、消费者处理消息两个阶段，任务的条件领取与结果写入承担重复投递下的一致性。 [006-reliable-judge.sql](../deploy/sql/006-reliable-judge.sql) · [JudgeOutboxMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeOutboxMapper.java) · [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

### 4. 技术取舍

直接让 Worker 轮询任务表也能异步执行，组件更少，但领取节奏、任务分发及数据库扫描压力都要自行处理。RabbitMQ 提供队列、预取和手动确认，代价是新增 Broker 和数据库到 Broker 的投递状态管理。当前 Outbox 用定时扫描，部署简单，也会引入扫描间隔和数据库查询。 [JudgeRecoveryJob.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/job/JudgeRecoveryJob.java) · [JudgeTaskConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/JudgeTaskConfig.java)

### 5. 局限与风险

发送预算有限，系统允许重复消息，也允许任务最终失败。前端的待确认请求保存在组件内，刷新页面后的同键恢复未实现。默认两秒轮询，慢请求可能重叠。实际吞吐、积压延迟及 Broker 高可用部署未确认。 [Answer.vue](../../../online-judge-friend-c/oj-c/src/views/Answer.vue) · [JudgeOutboxMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeOutboxMapper.java) · [application.yml](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/resources/application.yml)

## 二、面试问题与参考答案

### 1. 提交接口成功返回时，系统保证了什么？

它返回的是已持久化的任务编号。任务、执行快照和待发送事件在同一个受理事务里提交，任何一项失败都会回滚。此时判题可能尚未投递或执行，前端用编号查询。 [JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-friend/src/main/java/org/example/friend/service/user/impl/JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java) · [UserQuestionController.java](../oj-modules/oj-friend/src/main/java/org/example/friend/controller/user/UserQuestionController.java) · [Answer.vue](../../../online-judge-friend-c/oj-c/src/views/Answer.vue)

### 2. HTTP 响应丢了，两个同键请求还可能同时到达，怎么防重？

页面重试会复用原代码和原键。后端先按用户和键查询，相同摘要就返回原任务。两条请求都查不到时，唯一索引裁决。代码识别到该幂等索引冲突后，在创建事务回滚后重查，再核对摘要。同键改代码会报冲突。 [Answer.vue](../../../online-judge-friend-c/oj-c/src/views/Answer.vue) · [JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-friend/src/main/java/org/example/friend/service/user/impl/JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java) · [006-reliable-judge.sql](../deploy/sql/006-reliable-judge.sql)

### 3. 题目被修改后，重试为什么还能用原来的判题规则？

首次受理把拼装后的代码、用例和资源限制存入快照，消费者按任务编号读取。请求摘要包含题目、比赛、语言和原始代码，不包含题目版本。已受理的同键请求先回放旧任务，新的练习请求才校验题目版本，比赛受理读取发布时冻结的题目。 [JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-friend/src/main/java/org/example/friend/service/user/impl/JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

### 4. 为什么不在保存任务后直接发消息？

数据库提交成功后进程如果立刻退出，直接发送可能根本没发生。这里任务和事件一起提交，扫描器重启后还能发现待发送记录。网络发送放在工作线程中，数据库事务无需等待 Broker 确认。 [JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-friend/src/main/java/org/example/friend/service/user/impl/JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeRecoveryJob.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/job/JudgeRecoveryJob.java) · [OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java)

### 5. Publisher Confirm 收到 ACK，就可以把事件标为已发送吗？

代码同时要求 ACK 和没有 mandatory 路由退回。ACK 表明 Broker 确认了此次发布，但消息可能无法路由到目标队列。两项都满足才按本次领取令牌更新为 `SENT`，这个状态也只说明投递阶段完成。 [OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeOutboxMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeOutboxMapper.java) · [application.yml](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/resources/application.yml)

### 6. Broker 已收到消息，发布者却没收到确认，会发生什么？

发布者把它当成结果不确定，按原事件 ID 退避重发。若发布进程在写回前退出，扫描器会回收过期的 `INFLIGHT` 领取。队列可能出现重复消息，消费者通过派发代次和任务状态条件争夺执行权。 [OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeOutboxMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeOutboxMapper.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

### 7. 结果落库后还没 ACK，进程就挂了，会重复计分吗？

通道关闭后，未确认消息可能再次投递。任务已是 `FINISHED`，预检不会把它交给执行，消费者可确认这条重复投递。判题结果、提交历史和比赛成绩都在完成事务内，后续投递无法再次通过完成条件。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java)

### 8. MQ 长时间不可用时，用户提交最终会怎样？

受理只依赖数据库写入，发送会按预算退避。事件耗尽发送次数进入 `DEAD` 后，补偿只会终结仍在等同代次消息的任务。任务也可能先达到排队截止时间。任务失败可通过编号查询，不会无限自动发送。 [JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-friend/src/main/java/org/example/friend/service/user/impl/JudgeSubmissionServiceImpl.java) · [OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeOutboxMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeOutboxMapper.java) · [JudgeTaskMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeTaskMapper.java) · [application.yml](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/resources/application.yml)

### 9. 两个发布线程扫描到同一条事件，或者发布线程池已满，会怎样？

扫描拿到的是候选，工作线程必须先把到期 `PENDING` 条件更新成 `INFLIGHT`，成功才消耗发送次数。旧线程写回还要核对领取令牌。线程池满时本轮停止继续提交，被拒绝的候选尚未领取，后续扫描再处理。 [JudgeRecoveryJob.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/job/JudgeRecoveryJob.java) · [JudgeTaskConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/JudgeTaskConfig.java) · [OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/OutboxDeliveryServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeOutboxMapper.java](../oj-common/oj-common-mybatis/src/main/java/org/example/common/mybatis/mapper/JudgeOutboxMapper.java)
