# 判题资源治理

## 一、技术实现分析

### 1. 设计动机

判题并发受可用执行环境约束。先领任务再等容器会提前消耗执行租约。若只统计空闲容器，创建中、正在执行和待回收的环境都可能被漏算，导致反复补池并推高宿主占用。

### 2. 核心实现

1. `DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.createDockerSandBoxPool()` 以消费者并发数作正常容量，叠加回收余量作为硬上限。 `DockerSandBoxPool.initDockerPool()` 核对本 worker 的历史资源和归属明确的容器。 [DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java)
2. `JudgeRecoveryJob.replenish()` → `DockerSandBoxPool.replenish()` → `SandboxResourceServiceImpl.reserve()`，在 worker 行锁内计数并写 `RESERVED`。事务返回后 `SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.create()` 创建、连接并等 READY，随后入就绪队列。 [JudgeRecoveryJob.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/job/JudgeRecoveryJob.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java) · [SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java) · [SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java)
3. `JudgeConsumer.consume()` 经 `DockerSandBoxPool.pollContainer()` 出队并用 SQL 取得占用权，再领取任务。竞争失败且容器尚未执行，可用 `returnContainer()` 归还。用过的容器在 `finally` 退役。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java)
4. `JudgeRecoveryJob.recycle()` → `recoverRetired()` → `SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.recycle()`，核对容器、目录、连接和 cgroup 后由 SQL 结清。无资源或数据库异常时，`JudgeConsumer.pauseConsumption()` 异步停监听，`resumeConsumption()` 探测后恢复。 [JudgeRecoveryJob.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/job/JudgeRecoveryJob.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java) · [SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java) · [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java)

### 3. 技术原理

正常容量统计所有未关闭且非 `RECYCLING` 的资源，硬上限还统计待回收资源。持久化记录负责记账，内存队列只缓存本代次的就绪容器。数据库条件更新为资源占用提供最后一道检查。固定消费并发和每消费者预取 1 条控制在途投递，资源耗尽时进一步暂停入口。 [SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java) · [JudgeTaskConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/JudgeTaskConfig.java) · [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java)

### 4. 技术取舍

按请求临时建容器更直观，但创建和 READY 等待进入每次请求的执行路径。长期复用跑过用户代码的容器可减少创建次数，却要求可靠重置全部可变状态。这里预建一次性容器，用后退役，换取更简单的跨提交隔离边界，并承担持续创建与回收成本。按 worker 记账便于本地恢复，管理范围也随之限定。 [DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java) · [SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java)

### 5. 局限与风险

硬上限是 worker 的新建准入条件，多个 worker 共用宿主时缺少统一额度。worker 由配置名称前缀识别，同一 Docker 环境的并行实例若共用该标识，启动核对可能退役对方资源。创建结果长期未决会持续占额度。监听暂停影响同一监听器内的消费者，十秒探测间隔会带来恢复延迟。预热收益和宿主容量上限未实测。 [DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java) · [SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java) · [SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java) · [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java)

## 二、面试问题与参考答案

### 1. 为什么用两个容量上限？就绪队列有多少元素可以直接用来算吗？

就绪队列只看到可立即借用的容器。正常容量还计入预留中和已经借出的环境，硬上限再把回收中也算上。这样允许在回收期间用少量余量补池，又能阻止待回收资源不断挤占宿主。 [DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java) · [SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java)

### 2. 两个补池线程同时看到缺口，怎么防止同时超额创建？

`reserve()` 在同一短事务锁定 worker 行，读活动资源并判断两个上限。只有成功插入预留记录才进入外部创建。后一线程即使此前看到了空闲，拿锁后也会把新预留算进去。Docker 调用在事务提交之后，慢调用不需要持续占 worker 行锁。 [SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java)

### 3. 为什么拿到了容器还要再竞争任务？什么时候可以归还？

容器占用权和任务执行权是两次独立的条件更新。先拿资源，可以让任务租约从有执行条件时才开始。预检后若另一消费者抢先完成领取，当前消费者就归还尚未跑过用户代码的容器。用过的容器只走退役。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/JudgeTaskServiceImpl.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java)

### 4. Docker 创建调用超时，按名称暂时查不到容器，为什么不能释放名额？

请求可能已发出但结果未知，稍后仍可能出现容器。代码先记录创建阶段。能证明尚未发出 Docker 请求且前序状态可确认时，才按未创建处理。否则按唯一名称及标签核对，暂时找不到就保留名额。取舍是允许短期少用资源，避免未知的迟到创建让实际占用失控。 [SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java) · [SandboxCgroupServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxCgroupServiceImpl.java)

### 5. 停止容器后，数据库什么时候才真正释放容量？

停止或退役只是禁止继续使用。回收会确认容器删除并处理目录、连接和 cgroup，分别记录完成证据。只有四项齐全且创建不再未决，`close()` 才把记录改成 `CLOSED`。活动计数随后排除该记录。 [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java) · [SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxEnvironmentServiceImpl.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java)

### 6. 进程重启后，如何处理数据库里有记录或 Docker 里残留的容器？

启动时先把本 worker 未关闭的记录退役，再枚举容器并通过名称和标签核对归属。可确认的旧容器或孤儿容器补记进回收流程。新进程生成新的资源代次，领取 SQL 只接受该代次的 READY 记录。 [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java) · [DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java)

### 7. 没容器时为什么暂停监听，不一直 NACK 重入队？

反复重入队会在资源仍不可用时持续消耗投递和线程。当前等待到期后由另一个线程停止监听，相关分支先保留未确认状态。配置避免停止超时后强行关闭执行通道。实际重投依赖通道关闭后的 Broker 行为，代码没有主动循环 NACK。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [JudgeTaskConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/JudgeTaskConfig.java)

### 8. 暂停后检查什么？就绪检查通过是否等于已经拿到容器？

先用数据库候选查询做连通性探测，再查本地队列非空和旧消费者是否退出。条件不满足就安排十秒后重试，通过后重启监听。队列非空只是恢复信号，正式领取仍要出队并通过 `acquire()` 的状态和代次校验。 [JudgeConsumer.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/rabbit/JudgeConsumer.java) · [DockerSandBoxPool.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPool.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java)

### 9. 这个池能作为集群的全局资源调度器吗？

当前用 worker 作为计账范围，任务由 MQ 分发给监听者，容器队列只在本进程内。没有跨 worker 的统一宿主额度、负载调度或自动扩缩容。配置多个实例时，需要按部署环境设置不同的资源归属标识，并单独规划宿主容量。 [DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/DockerSandBoxPoolConfig.java) · [SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/service/impl/SandboxResourceServiceImpl.java) · [SandboxResourceMapper.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/mapper/SandboxResourceMapper.java) · [JudgeTaskConfig.java](../oj-modules/oj-judge/src/main/java/org/example/judge/config/JudgeTaskConfig.java)
